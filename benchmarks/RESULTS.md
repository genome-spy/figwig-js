# Historical benchmark results

The [HTTP measurements and optimization experiments](https://github.com/genome-spy/figwig-js/blob/b0bac083052deaf0a5a3f6d61e200967909c3c79/benchmarks/RESULTS.md)
and their [raw samples](https://github.com/genome-spy/figwig-js/tree/b0bac083052deaf0a5a3f6d61e200967909c3c79/benchmarks/measurements)
are preserved at the pre-cleanup revision `b0bac08`.
They describe the reader revisions, workloads and environments recorded there;
they are not current-version performance guarantees.

See [README.md](README.md) for maintained benchmark commands and measurement
boundaries. New runs write to ignored `benchmarks/results/`; summarize relevant
results in the commit body, PR description or changeset, following
[the evidence policy](../AGENTS.md#documentation-and-evidence).
